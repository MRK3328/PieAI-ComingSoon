Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

At Pie AI, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information. This Privacy Policy explains how data is handled when you interact with our platform.

1. Information We Collect

  • Basic account information when restaurants register.
  • Usage data related to interactions with the Pie AI assistant.
  • Technical data such as browser type for performance optimization.

2. How Information Is Used

  • To operate and improve Pie AI services.
  • To provide customer and restaurant support.
  • To maintain system security and reliability.

3. Data Security

We apply industry-standard safeguards to protect data. Information is never sold or shared with third parties unless legally required.

4. User Control

Restaurants may request updates or removal of their data at any time by contacting Pie AI support.

5. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated periodically. Continued use of Pie AI indicates acceptance of revisions.
