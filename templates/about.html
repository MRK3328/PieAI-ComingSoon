Our mission

Pie AI

Building a safer table for everyone

Pie AI exists to modernize the restaurant industry through intelligent automation. Our focus is simple safer dining, clearer communication, and reduced operational strain.

By delivering real time, accurate menu and allergen information, Pie AI improves accessibility for guests with allergies while minimizing reliance on staff for routine questions.

The result is a safer guest experience, a more efficient restaurant, and dining that feels confident, inclusive, and effortless.

Origin

Where it All
Began

Pie AI began as a simple idea shaped by firsthand experience inside real restaurants. Starting work at a local restaurant at fifteen, I moved through nearly every role — from busser to server to bartender.

Over time, one problem became clear: allergy information was often inconsistent, difficult to access, and dependent on rushed staff — creating real risk for guests and businesses alike.

With a background in business and a drive to solve real problems, I taught myself to code and built Pie AI from the ground up. Through constant testing and refinement, it evolved into a platform designed to improve safety, efficiency, and clarity across the dining experience.
