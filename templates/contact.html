Common questions
Quick Answers
How do I upload my menu?
Go to Files → Food Menus or Drink Menu, tap upload, and select your Excel or PDF. The AI will read it instantly.
Can customers see my restaurant without signing up?
Yes — no login needed. They scan your QR code or search and chat directly with no friction.
How do I update allergy information?
Upload or edit your Allergy Files, or use Item Search to update specific dishes directly from your dashboard.
Is my data private?
Yes — each restaurant has its own sealed data container. Nothing transfers between accounts.
